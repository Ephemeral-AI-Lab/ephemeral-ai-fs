Three repetitions per cell are three repeated measurements of one selected file/range, not three independently sampled workloads. There are five selected strata, not60 independent file examples. Report all three raw observations, their median and min/max, and paired absolute differences in the predeclared order. The maximum is **largest observed latency**, not a worst-case guarantee; avoid p95/p99, significance or population-equivalence claims.
