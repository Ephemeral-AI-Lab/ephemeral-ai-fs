cache warming and observer duration are separate; no clone allocation or OS-cold
claim. Partition SQLite pages once; separate actual BLOBs, row encoding, index and
metadata pages, payload/unused/overhead, freelist and residual. Signed allocation

inventory kinds. All selected IDs, logical roots, transitive physical bases and

bases belong in closure. Changed structural group results are measured interaction,
not an assumed offlineconstant. No proportional v1 compressed-record attribution.

invocation scopes, host/container CPU/RSS/I/O and temporary/free space honestly.
Public/whole-operation cost is not a sum of nested matcher/codec times. Historical
D/C/S1/Git results remain historical context, not new timing pairs. Fresh one-pair
observations are not repeatable speedup or release statistics. Read coverage and
outliers above remain part of disposition even if full throughput looks better.

resource or cleanup failure; preserve partial evidence. Correct demonstrated
integration defects only with explicit new identities and retained failed evidence.
